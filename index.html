Learn Amazon Quick with Phoebe

Amazon Quick puts dashboards, chat over documents, chat over data, a research agent and workflow automation into one AWS product with one per-user price. This crash course is for the person who has to decide whether to buy it: what it is and how its parts fit, what it really costs and where your data goes while it answers, and a bench that computes Quick against an in-house knowledge platform and checks your must-haves Region by Region. Facts read on AWS pages on 26 November 2026; the product moves fast, so every page says where to re-check.

The three sessions ⚡ · from the demo to the decision memo

The running case is Oakmere Insurance, a constructed regional insurer in Singapore. Its data team built Compass, an in-house knowledge platform inside Oakmere's own AWS account: a governed lake, personal-data rules, a chatbot, text-to-SQL and an MCP server for AI agents. AWS has offered Amazon Quick. Priya, the head of data, has three sessions to decide. Oakmere and everyone in it are invented for teaching, and every page says so.
